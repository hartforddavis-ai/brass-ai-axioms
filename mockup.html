The Shearpin Process

Four instruments that show where AI-written code leaves off doing what you asked.

Near enough ships. Then the clockwork jams.
Shearpin marks the exact tooth where it slips.

  • The Brass Axioms Live Three axioms your agent can't drift, pad, or counterfeit past. One paste. GitHub →
  • Blacksmith Live Forges the build from your prompt, every step riveted to the spec — drift shows at step 3, not step 40. GitHub →
  • VibeValve Soon Holds the agent's own fix to your order, clause by clause, before it passes.
  • Gauge Soon Passes only what reads true on the gauge; every unproven claim it holds back.
